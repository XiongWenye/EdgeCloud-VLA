# Research gap

The verified evidence does not establish whether CloudEdgeVLA's representational specialization survives a generative flow-matching action expert. The measurable question is whether dual fresh/stale flow supervision improves success and delay-retention over a single-path π0.5 checkpoint at matched `d_max`, while preserving synchronous success.

Required baselines are synchronous π0.5, delayed single-path π0.5, and CloudEdge π0.5. Required metrics are suite success, normalized delay AUC, retention at 40 steps, cloud-context drift, action drift, wall-clock cloud/edge latency, GPU memory, and network payload size. Orin/Thor power and latency remain an unclosed deployment gap.
